## Analysis and deliverables

### From exchange data to bilingual exhibits

I organised volume, turnover and open interest by exchange, product and reporting period. I checked QFI and internationalised product coverage, aligned names, tickers and units, and distinguished period trading activity from a point-in-time open interest measure.

### Build two 14-slide QFI decks

The Chinese and English versions covered the market, product access, comparable offshore contracts, account opening, execution and risk controls. I turned Excel tables into presentation exhibits for the team’s China market briefings.

### Research global institutions and industrial clients

I prepared briefs on firms including StoneX and Marex, mapping business lines such as brokerage and clearing, geographic coverage and links to China to inform internal discussions on potential collaboration.
