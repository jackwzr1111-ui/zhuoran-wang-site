## Analysis and deliverables

### Map indicators to the value chain

Monthly output, trade and steel mill inventories show longer trends. Daily futures, spot prices and freight inform spread analysis. The framework identifies Australian and Brazilian shipments, Chinese port arrivals, hot metal output and port stocks as weekly cross-checks.

### Make assumptions explicit

The workbook tracks the HRC–rebar spread, rebar–iron ore ratio, coke margins, basis and futures curves. Its rebar margin proxy subtracts 1.6 times ore, 0.5 times coke and other costs from rebar futures, with editable factors on an assumptions sheet.

### Design for updates

Wind EDB feeds the monthly inputs and daily prices can be archived as values. Seasonality and percentile charts preserve gaps. Comparing Dalian and Singapore iron ore requires aligning currency, dry and wet tonnes, and price basis.
