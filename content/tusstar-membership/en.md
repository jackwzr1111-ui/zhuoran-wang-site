## Member register · Workflow illustration

How a member record changes as work moves forward.

### Intake

**Under review**

Record the lead source, market direction and business need. Assign an owner.

### Review & onboarding

**Awaiting payment → Active**

After review and payment confirmation, record dates and the contract reference.

### Matching

**Matching in progress**

Link the member to the proposed partner and record meeting notes and the next action.

### Follow-up & renewal

**Renewal due**

Check in 60, 30 and 7 days before expiry, using the record of benefits delivered.

## Analysis and deliverables

### Reconcile the records before redesigning benefits

I reviewed historical reports, member lists and benefits, flagging possible duplicates, membership status and payment records for checks. The proposal defined three tracks for entering Malaysia, entering China and ecosystem partners, with matching, events and soft-landing services.

### Build the management site

The site groups the overview, member register, intake, matching requests, events, benefits and import/export. The register tracks status, expiry and recent contact, while the dashboard assembles follow-ups and monthly reporting figures.

### Define roles, deadlines and handoffs

Version 1.0 contains 12 SOPs, covering intake, review and payment, onboarding, matching, events, community operations, renewal and reporting. A matching request runs from initial recording and shortlisting to meeting notes and follow-up.

### Support China–Malaysia industry events

I joined the Chengdu–Malaysia science and technology matchmaking meeting and AIFTIS & CMIIE 2026. I prepared event briefs and recorded follow-up topics around innovation, smart manufacturing and entering overseas markets.
